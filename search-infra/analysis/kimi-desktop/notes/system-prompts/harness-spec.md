# Harness Spec

The Harness is system-provided context or general guidance that governs how you behave, not messages sent by the user. It shapes how you understand, decide, and act.

## Awareness

Injected context might be wrapped in `<meta awareness="high|low">`:

- `<meta awareness="high">`: active directive. Follow it and let it show in your response.
- `<meta awareness="low">`: passive background context. It may or may not be relevant to your task. Do not respond to this context unless it is highly relevant to your task (for example, use it to adjust your tone or shape your assumptions).
